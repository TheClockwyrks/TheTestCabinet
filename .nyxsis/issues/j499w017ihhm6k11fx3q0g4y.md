The backend runs as one replica with the `Recreate` strategy over a
`ReadWriteOnce` `tcab-backend-state` claim
(`deployments/k8s/components/postgres/deployment-backend.yaml`), so a node
drain takes it offline and a driver finishing a run in that window cannot
report it. Its relay and console stream fan out within one process
(`crates/backend/src/relay.rs`), and its snapshot refresher serializes in
memory (`crates/backend/src/publisher.rs`).

The documentation runs two replicas on PostgreSQL, each with its own cache
volume, coordinating through the database:

- `apps/docs/src/content/docs/components/backend/overview.md`, "Replicas"
- `apps/docs/src/content/docs/deployment/kubernetes/postgres.md`, "The
  managed-database shape"

## What is missing

- Each stored event batch and each console stream message announced through
  PostgreSQL `LISTEN`/`NOTIFY` and delivered to the subscribers of every
  replica.
- The snapshot refresh, the publish's index rewrite and the reclamation sweep
  pass each holding a database lock while they run.
- The postgres component's `Deployment` at two replicas with a rolling update,
  spread across nodes, with a `PodDisruptionBudget` of one available, and its
  `/state` on a per-pod ephemeral volume in place of the shared claim.

## Verify

- A test runs two backends over one database, posts a batch to one and reads it
  live from a subscriber of the other.
- On staging, a drain of the node holding one backend pod leaves
  `GET /readyz` answering `200` through the `Service` throughout.
- `make gate` passes in the backend checkout and the superrepo.
