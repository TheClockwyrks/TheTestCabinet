The console app lives in the superrepo as `apps/web`. The documentation
places it in the `console.testcabinet.ai` repository, a site depending on
`ui-common` and the packages `contracts` generates, built into the `tcab-web`
image: `apps/docs/src/content/docs/components/web/overview.md`, section
"Deployment", and `apps/docs/src/content/docs/development/repositories.md`,
sections "The repositories", "The edges" and "How a dependency is named".

## What is missing

The `console.testcabinet.ai` repository on Azure, populated with the app's
history, rendered by the kit with its `typescript` gate, `build-context` and
pipeline, pushing `tcab-web:<commit>`, mirrored to GitHub, and pinned by the
superrepo's deploy sets. The app is removed from the superrepo.

## Verify

`make gate` passes in the new repository and in the superrepo, and a staging
deploy serves the console at the origin from the image at the submodule
pointer.
