**Floe Berg** is a hazard of the Floe arctic-crossing game — a jagged chunk of
drifting ice that slides across the lanes the player must cross. Because it has
to
read as a hard, dangerous hazard to dodge and stay clearly distinct from the flat,
safe floe the player stands on, it is its own asset-generation case: a model draws
it as a **single 32×32 sprite**, one operation at a time — a jagged, faceted,
three-dimensional block of ice with sharp top points, shaded facets, dark cracks,
and snow highlights, centered on full transparency. The recorded operations are
regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges against the brief: the read
as
a hard, sharp, dangerous berg (not a flat floe), the faceted three-dimensional
form, and the cold ice palette are what they weigh.
