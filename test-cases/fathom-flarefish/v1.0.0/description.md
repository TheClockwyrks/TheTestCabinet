**Fathom Flarefish** is a deep-sea predator that is blind between flares and hunts in
the light of its own bright bloom, one of the chasers in a lightless maze-chase
game. This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a **sprite sheet**
using only the drawing tool, one operation at a time: 8 separate 32×32 frames
holding a four-direction swim cycle.

The creature carries its dim flare organ on its body, the brightest fixed mark on
the orange silhouette — but the flare bloom itself, which lights a far larger
radius than the sprite, is a separate effect asset (the Fathom Flare Bloom case),
not drawn here. The recorded operations are regenerated into each frame, which a
reviewer judges against the brief: the flare organ, the four readable directions,
and the orange palette are what they weigh, and the named sequences play back as
live animations in the review UI.
