**Wireworm Dropper** is the packet-dropper hazard of the Wireworm circuit-board
arcade game — the data packet that falls straight down the board, seeding fresh
capacitor nodes in its wake when the field thins. Because it has to read as a
falling packet in motion, it is its own asset-generation case: a model draws
it as
a **single 32×32 sprite**, one operation at a time — a compact amber data parcel
with a down-chevron glyph, rising speed-line trails, and a leading glow, symmetric
about its center and clearly dropping downward on full transparency. The recorded
operations are regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges against the
brief: the downward-motion read, the warm-amber hazard palette, and the data-packet
form are what they weigh.
