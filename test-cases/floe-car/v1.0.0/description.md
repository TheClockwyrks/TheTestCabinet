**Floe Car** is a hazard of the Floe arctic-crossing game — an ordinary sedan
driving across the lanes the player must thread, a third vehicle alongside the big
slow snow plow and the fast sled-dog team. Because it has to read as a normal car
to dodge (never a creature or a safe platform), it is its own asset-generation
case: a model draws it as a **single 64×32 (two-tile) sprite**, one operation at a
time — a top-down sedan with a rounded body, a glass cabin, four wheels, and its
head- and taillights, facing right on full transparency. The recorded operations
are regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges against the brief: the
read as a plain car, the top-down sedan detailing, and the car palette are what
they weigh.
