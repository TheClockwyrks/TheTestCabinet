**Floe Crawler** is a hazard of the Floe arctic-crossing game — a heavy tracked
snow-crawler that grinds back and forth across the ice lanes the player must
thread. Because it has to read instantly as a dangerous machine to dodge (never
a
creature or a safe platform), it is its own asset-generation case: a model draws
it
as a **single 32×32 sprite**, one operation at a time — a hard-edged steel crawler
on caterpillar treads, with a glass-windowed cab, hazard-yellow stripes on its
leading edge, and a red warning beacon, facing right on full transparency. The
recorded operations are regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges
against the brief: the read as a hard industrial hazard, the mechanical detailing,
and the industrial palette are what they weigh.
