**Wireworm Cursor** is the player's craft in the Wireworm circuit-board arcade
game — the defrag cursor that patrols the bottom band and fires upward at the
descending data-worm. Because it must read instantly as the player's own ship
rather than one of the hostile bugs, it is its own asset-generation case: a model
draws it as a **single 32×32 sprite**, one operation at a time — a symmetric,
upward-pointing delta craft with a glowing reactor core, a muzzle at its tip,
selection-caret corner ticks, and a thruster underglow, in a cool cyan-and-white
palette on full transparency. The recorded operations are regenerated into the
sprite, which a reviewer judges against the brief: the upward-pointing player read,
the friendly cursor motif, and the tight palette are what they weigh.
