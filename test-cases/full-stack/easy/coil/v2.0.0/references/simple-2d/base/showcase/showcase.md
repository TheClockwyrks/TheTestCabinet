# Coil

Coil is a neon grid serpent. One snake threads a single continuous path across a
bordered grid, and it never stops: you steer it, and the turn lands on the next
step. It can only turn across the way it is travelling, so it can never double
back into its own neck, and every route you take is a route you have to leave
yourself room to get out of.

Every pellet you eat makes the snake one cell longer. That is the whole of the
pressure. Early on the board is open and a pellet is a stroll away; forty cells
later the same pellet sits behind a wall of your own body, and the question is
no longer where it is but which way around it you can afford to go.

The combo is what turns survival into a score. Eat a pellet and a window opens;
eat the next one before it runs out and the multiplier climbs, up to five times.
Dawdle and it falls back to one and you start again. So the strong player is not
the careful one, it is the one who reads the next two pellets at once and takes
the line that reaches both.

A round ends the moment the head touches the wall border or its own body. There
is no grace tick and no second chance. Clear the board and you win outright,
which almost nobody does.
