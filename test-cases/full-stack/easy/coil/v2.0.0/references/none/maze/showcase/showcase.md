# Coil

Coil is a neon grid serpent, played here across a course of bars laid through
the middle of the board. One snake threads a single continuous path, and it
never stops: you steer it, and the turn lands on the next step. It can only turn
across the way it is travelling, so it can never double back into its own neck.

The bars are as fatal as the wall border, and they never move. What they take
away is the easy diagonal: a pellet three cells off is often eight cells of
travel, because the straight line runs through a bar. Learning the course is
half of playing well here, and the other half is not walling yourself in with
your own body while you go around it.

Every pellet you eat makes the snake one cell longer, so the room the bars left
you keeps shrinking. Early on there is a way around everything; forty cells
later the gap you were counting on is full of your own tail.

The combo is what turns survival into a score. Eat a pellet and a window opens;
eat the next one before it runs out and the multiplier climbs, up to five times.
Dawdle and it falls back to one. On this board that is a real gamble, because
the fast line and the safe line are rarely the same line.

A round ends the moment the head touches a wall, a bar, or its own body. There
is no grace tick and no second chance.
