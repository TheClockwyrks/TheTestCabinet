# Kessler

A planet sits at the center of the field, and three rings of derelict
satellites circle it, each turning at its own speed and direction. They are
slated for demolition, and you are the crew: from a low track just above the
planet you ride the deflector, batting a demolition ball outward into the
rings until every derelict is swept from the sky.

![Mid-wave, the shield armed and a salvage pod falling](mid-wave.png)

The deflector is your only tool and your only defense. Where the ball lands on
its arc steers the bounce — meet it square and the ball flies straight back
out, catch it on the edge and it whips away at an angle — and every bounce off
the deflector restores the ball to full speed. A ball you miss burns up
against the planet, and losing your last ball with no lives left ends the run.

Breaking a derelict sometimes shakes loose a salvage pod, which falls slowly
toward the planet. Catch one on the deflector for a tool: a wider deflector, a
second ball, a shield around the planet, a piercing ball that destroys
whatever it touches, or — beware — a narrower deflector. Every catch scores,
but the narrow pod is a trade you may not want.

Clear all three rings and the wave ends with a bonus, and the next wave comes
back faster and worth more. There is no final wave; Kessler is a score attack,
and the only question is how long you can keep the sky falling.
