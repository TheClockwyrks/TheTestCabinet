Introduced. A gem-matching puzzle on a lapidary's lit bench: an `8x8` board of
cut stones where swapping two neighbors lines up three or more of a kind, the
stones above fall into the gap, and fresh ones drop in from the top, so one swap
can touch off a chain that resolves long after the player's hand has left the
board. What the round is really made of is strain: every clear presses on the
gems left standing around it, that pressure is never relieved, and a gem cracked
through clears with anything that clears beside it and is worth double, so the
board is always either a resource being primed or one about to be spent. Three
cuts sit on top of it — a run of four leaving a brilliant that takes its
neighbors, two runs crossing leaving a star that takes a whole row and column,
and a run of five or more leaving a prism that takes every stone of the kind
it is traded against — and the round climbs a level at a time until no legal swap is
left. The build produces the game's art, effects, and sound itself during the
run, across the engineless run, the [Simple 2D](/engines/simple-2d/) engine, and
the [Structured 2D](/engines/structured-2d/) engine, on a single `base` variant.
