Warhead is Shatter with armor on the rocks and a heavy weapon on the ship. The
field is the same wrapping stretch of deep space with a star burning at the centre
of it, and you fly the same small ship under pure momentum — turn, thrust, no
brake, and off one edge is straight back in at the other.

The star still bends everything except the ship. Rocks and bullets alike fall
toward it, so shots curve as they cross the middle of the field and can be swung
right around the star onto a rock on its far side. Its core swallows shots and
throws rocks back out from the edge of the field.

What is different is that a rock no longer breaks on one hit. A Large takes three,
a Medium two, and every hit that leaves a rock standing flashes it and leaves it
visibly more broken, so you can read how much is left in it. That makes a wave a
much longer fight — and it is why the ship carries a torpedo: one guided munition
that locks onto whatever is in a narrow cone ahead of it, flies dead straight
through the well, and destroys any rock outright however much armor it had left,
blasting the fragments apart as it goes. Spend it and you spend ten seconds
watching the bar under the HUD fill before you have another.

The saucer arrives to hunt all the same, weaving across the field and taking an
aimed shot at you every second and a half while you are busy chewing through the
wave.

The clip is half a minute of the reference build, played from the title screen:
armored rocks worn down under the gun, torpedo runs launched and detonated, and
the saucer arriving in the middle of it.
