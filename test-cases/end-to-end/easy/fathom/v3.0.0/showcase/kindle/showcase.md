Fathom is a dive into a flooded, pitch-dark trench. You are a small glowing
forager threading its corridors, grazing the plankton drifting there while three
predators hunt the signals you give off: one tracks your light, one hunts your
sound, and one shows nothing of itself between the flares it casts. Your own
light travels straight and stops at rock, so it shows you a pocket and no more.
A sonar pulse floods the corridors instead, bending around every corner to find
what waits past the bend, and something down there is listening for it. Ink is
the only thing you carry that a hunter answers to.

The Kindle dive is Fathom seen through a window. You carry a circle that widens
as you eat, and beyond its edge even corridors you have already mapped go dark,
remembered but unlit until you swim back to them. Eating brightens you, which
opens the window and lights the way, and that same glow is what the hunters
track: the faster you feed, the further into the dark they can find you.

The replay is a full dive of about thirty-four seconds, from the title card
through the countdown into live play. The trench opens a few tiles at a time,
sonar washes down the corridors, a Gloamfin's ping sweeps back through them, a
Flarefish charges and blooms, ink goes down as a hunter closes, a bonus drifter
is run down for the amber it pays, and the window widens as the score climbs.
All of it was captured from the reference implementation in live play.
