Fathom is a maze chase played in the dark, at the bottom of a trench. You are a
small bioluminescent forager threading flooded corridors, grazing the plankton
that drifts in them while three hunters work the same passages. Nothing down
here is lit for you. You see the pocket your own glow reaches, you remember the
ground you have already crossed, and everything past that is black water.

The sonar pulse is how you look around a corner. It floods the corridors instead
of spreading as a circle, so it bends through junctions, lights the rock it
breaks against, and paints whatever it sweeps over. It is also loud, and one of
the three hunters is listening for exactly that.

Eating is the trade the whole dive turns on. Every mote makes you brighter, a
brighter forager sees more of the maze, and a brighter forager is seen further
off by the two hunters that hunt by light. You carry no weapon and cannot turn
the hunt around. Ink is all you have: a cloud dropped in a corridor blinds the
two that hunt by sight and buys you the corner you need.

On the Standard dive the whole of the trench you have explored stays drawn, dim,
and the amber lights out in the dark carry to you at any distance. One of them
is a harmless drifter worth twenty mouthfuls. One of them is the Lanternjaw. You
will not know which until it turns.
