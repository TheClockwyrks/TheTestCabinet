Carom is a top-down paddle duel: two paddles trade a ball across a walled
field with two obstacles planted mid-court, and a paddle's motion at the
moment of contact puts spin on the ball, curving its flight afterward. The
base variant serves a single ball toward the receiver over fixed, upright
obstacles, and play runs to 11, win by two. The replay is half a minute of a
real Solo match against the reference implementation's AI — swept returns
curving off their lines, wall banks, obstacle ricochets, saves at full
stretch, and two points taken — captured from the reference implementation
in live play.
