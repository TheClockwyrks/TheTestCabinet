# Carom

Carom is a paddle duel played across a single screen, and this version plays it
with three balls at once. Two paddles face each other down a long field, two
obstacles stand in the middle, and three balls run between them at the same time.

The paddles do more than block. A paddle that is moving when the ball arrives puts
spin on it, and a spinning ball curves as it travels, so where you meet a ball
matters as much as whether you reach it.

Three balls change what you are doing entirely. They collide with one another, so
a shot you played cleanly can be knocked off line by a ball you were not watching.
A point takes one ball out of play and sends it back to its own home while the
other two carry on, so the field never stops and you are never only defending one
thing.

Play Solo against the computer or Versus a friend on the same keyboard. First to
eleven, win by two.
