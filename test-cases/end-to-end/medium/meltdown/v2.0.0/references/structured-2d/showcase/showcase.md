# Meltdown

Meltdown is a tower defense fought on the floor of a reactor, and the towers are
the walls. Surge intruders pour in through vents cut in the casing and run for
the exhausts on the far side. There is no lane: the route they walk is whatever
route your guns leave them, so every wall you put down is a gun and every gun you
put down is a wall.

Heat is where the power is. An emitter fires harder the hotter it runs and climbs
to full damage at its own redline. The scale does not stop there, and a gun
carried all the way to the top of it trips offline to cool, leaving a hole in
your line. A tower sheds heat only through the faces that touch open air, so the
tighter you pack the floor the harder your guns cook. Two support structures
sculpt it: a Forge that holds everything it touches near a setpoint, and a Sink
that drains a core with nowhere else left to lose heat.

Six emitters test the same idea different ways: the balanced Arc, the twitchy
fast-tripping Stutter, the huge Lance that runs cold until you tuck it away, the
splashing Bloom, the air-only Flak, and the cryo Rime, which runs the rule
backwards and slows hardest while it stays cool.

Five modes are on the menu: Containment at three difficulties, a single
hundred-strong onslaught, a flush start, a restricted central build zone, and a
one-life sudden death.
