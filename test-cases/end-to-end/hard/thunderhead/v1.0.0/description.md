**Thunderhead** is a real-time, combined-arms fleet-command game for the
browser, fought over a procedurally generated world of mountainous islands
drowned in an endless cloud sea. You command a fleet of sky-warships, aircraft,
and cloud-diving submersibles against an enemy fleet for command of the sky.
Destroying the enemy flagship wins the battle; losing your own ends it.

Its signature is that you play at two scales at once. A tilted tactical command
view orders your whole fleet across the islands. At any instant you can drop
into direct control of any unit you own, and any weapon station aboard it, then
pull back out to command while the rest of your fleet fights on under your
orders. Steering is always yours, and the guns you are not manning keep firing
themselves. What you can take over directly:

- A battleship's guns, laid by class: every turret bearing and in range fires at
  one crosshair, and each turret and barrel reloads on its own clock.
- A fighter, flown yourself.
- A bomber's turrets, manned yourself.
- A submersible, dived into the murk.

The terrain drives the fighting. Mountainous islands and floating crags break
the cloud sea, and the dense murk below the cloud line is where submersibles
hide. Ridges and islands block sight and gunfire, high ground sees farther, and
deep valleys give room to dive.

Three asymmetric powers fight over it, each with its own roster, mechanics, and
playstyle, all player-selectable and AI-commanded:

- The industrial Ironbound, with gunpowder artillery, armor, and damage-control
  crews.
- The elegant Meridian, with high-velocity guns, energy shields, and blinking
  aircraft.
- The crystalline Geode, with resonance beams and a healing web projected by
  support ships.

Thunderhead is inspired by combined-arms naval-command games but is its own
game, with an original name, powers, world, and rosters. It is the catalog's
largest build:

- A procedurally generated 3D world under an animated cloud sea.
- A fleet of provided rigid models rendered and articulated in real time.
- Two-scale command-and-possession control across surface, air, and murk.
- A class-based gunnery model with per-turret and per-barrel simulation.
- A damage model of armor, shields, resonance, fire, and flooding.
- Detection and a fog of war.
- A reinforcement economy.
- Three asymmetric powers and an AI opponent.
