**Siege** is a first-person, voxel last-stand survival shooter for the browser.
You are a Warden defending a chain of three fortified redoubts, A, B, and C,
strung across a procedurally generated frontier, while the Scourge, a red
mechanized tide, pours in from the far edge to overrun them. You cannot win by
holding: the assault only escalates. Every redoubt is ground down and falls,
you fall back to the next, and when the last one breaks the siege is over. You
play for how long you survive and how many attackers you destroy.

You fight on foot as one of three classes: a Ranger with an automatic rifle, a
Marksman with an armor-piercing long rifle, or a Breacher with an arcing splash
launcher. Each has a primary, a secondary, and a grenade, and the class is
chosen at the in-game spawn UI and re-chosen on every respawn. A four-Warden
squad fights beside you: a rifleman, a machine gunner, a medic, and an
engineer. Only the medic can heal and only the engineer resupplies ammo, and
there are no pickups.

The Scourge escalate on two axes. As your kill count climbs the same attackers
spawn at tougher, up-armored tiers, and each new redoubt introduces a new kind
of attacker. Dedicated breaker sappers and arcing artillery grind each redoubt
down, and the artillery lays ground telegraphs you must dodge.

Siege is a hard build. It asks for a procedurally generated voxel world
rendered in real-time 3D, a first-person pointer-lock controller, hitscan,
projectile, and arcing weapons, two independent 3D-pathfinding AI systems, a
health-based objective with a fighting-retreat survival loop, and multiple
states. It draws on objective-defense and horde survival shooters, with an
original name, factions, world, and rosters.
