**Siege** is a first-person, voxel **last-stand survival** shooter for the browser.
You are a Warden defending a chain of three fortified redoubts — **A, B, and C** —
strung across a procedurally generated frontier, while the **Scourge**, a red
mechanized tide, pours in from the far edge to overrun them. You cannot win by
holding: the assault only escalates. Every redoubt is ground down and falls, you
fall back to the next, and when the last one breaks the siege is over. What you
play for is **how long you survive** and **how many attackers you destroy**.

You fight on foot as one of three **classes** — a Ranger with an automatic rifle, a
Marksman with an armor-piercing long rifle, or a Breacher with an arcing splash
launcher — each with a primary, secondary, and grenade, chosen (and re-chosen on
every respawn) at the in-game spawn UI. A four-Warden **squad** — a rifleman, a
machine gunner, a medic, and an engineer — fights beside you: **only the medic can
heal** and **only the engineer resupplies ammo**, with no pickups. The Scourge
escalate on two axes: as your **kill count** climbs the same attackers spawn at
tougher, up-armored **tiers**, and each new redoubt introduces a **new kind of
attacker** — while dedicated **breaker** sappers and arcing **artillery** (with
ground telegraphs you must dodge) grind each redoubt down no matter how well you
shoot.

Siege is a hard build: a procedurally generated voxel world rendered in real-time
3D, a first-person pointer-lock controller, hitscan, projectile, and arcing
weapons, two independent 3D-pathfinding AI systems, a health-based objective with a
fighting-retreat survival loop, and multiple states. It is inspired by
objective-defense and horde survival shooters but is its own game, with an original
name, factions, world, and rosters.
