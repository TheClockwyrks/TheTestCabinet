**Thunderhead Theme** is the main-menu music for *Thunderhead*, a real-time,
combined-arms **fleet-command** game fought across a cloud sea.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a long, grand, cinematic
**main-title overture** — the sweeping piece that plays over the title screen —
using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time. It is 3-to-5 minutes
long, must **develop** across that length rather than loop one idea, is **not**
tied to any single power, and must loop cleanly under the menu. The mood is a
cold, high war over an endless cloud sea: evocative, serious, and stirring, voiced
as notes on instrument tracks the model chooses freely from the baked `gm-lite`
instrument bank. The recorded operations are regenerated into the clip (a stereo
`clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`), which a reviewer plays against the brief
— its grandeur, its development over the piece, and its clean loop are what they
weigh.
