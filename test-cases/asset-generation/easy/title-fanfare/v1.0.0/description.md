**Upbeat Title Theme** is a short main-menu cue for a casual game — the bright,
inviting music that plays under the title screen while the player waits to press
start.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, clean-looping
clip using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: a **bright, bouncy,
inviting** theme with a playful, skipping melody over cheerful strings, light
plucks, and a peppy drum bounce, building to a small triumphant lift — energetic but
light. The mood and role are prescribed; the key, tempo, structure, and instrument
choices are the model's, so the case rewards compositional creativity. It is voiced
as notes on instrument tracks the model chooses freely from the baked `gm-lite`
instrument bank. The recorded operations are regenerated into the clip (a stereo
`clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`), which a reviewer plays against the brief —
its brightness, its bounce, and its little triumphant lift are what they weigh.
