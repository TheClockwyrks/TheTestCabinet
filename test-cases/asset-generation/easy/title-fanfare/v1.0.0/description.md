**Upbeat Title Theme** is a short main-menu cue for a casual game, the bright,
inviting music that plays under the title screen while the player waits to press
start.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, clean-looping
clip using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time. The brief calls
for a bright, bouncy, inviting theme: a playful, skipping melody over cheerful
strings, light plucks, and a peppy drum bounce, building to a small triumphant
lift, energetic but light. The mood and role are prescribed, and the key, tempo,
structure, and instrument choices are the model's, so the case rewards
compositional creativity. The cue is voiced as notes on instrument tracks the
model chooses freely from the baked `gm-lite` instrument bank.

The recorded operations are regenerated into the clip, a stereo `clip.wav`
beside a portable `clip.mid`. A reviewer plays that clip against the brief and
weighs its brightness, its bounce, and its little triumphant lift.
