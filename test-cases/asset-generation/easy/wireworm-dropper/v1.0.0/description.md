**Wireworm Dropper** is the packet-dropper hazard of the Wireworm circuit-board
arcade game, the data packet that falls straight down the board and seeds fresh
capacitor nodes in its wake when the field thins.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a single 32×32 sprite, one
operation at a time: a compact amber data parcel with a down-chevron glyph,
rising speed-line trails, and a leading glow, symmetric about its center and
clearly dropping downward on full transparency.

The recorded operations are regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges
against the brief. The downward-motion read, the warm-amber hazard palette, and
the data-packet form are what they weigh.
