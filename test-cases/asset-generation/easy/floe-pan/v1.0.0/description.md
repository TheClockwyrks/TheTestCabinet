**Floe Pan** is the safe platform of the Floe arctic-crossing game — a flat pan
of
drifting ice the player hops onto to cross the open water. Because it has to
read as
calm, flat, solid footing (and stay clearly distinct from the jagged, dangerous
ice
hazard), it is its own asset-generation case: a model draws it as a **single 32×32
sprite**, one operation at a time — a smooth, rounded, tile-filling ice pan with
a
snow-dusted top surface, a ringed edge, and a waterline shadow so it reads as
floating, on full transparency. The recorded operations are regenerated into the
sprite, which a reviewer judges against the brief: the read as flat, calm, safe
footing (not a hazard), the floating waterline, and the pale ice palette are what
they weigh.
