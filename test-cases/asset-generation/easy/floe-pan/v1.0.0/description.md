**Floe Pan** is the safe platform of the Floe arctic-crossing game, a flat pan
of drifting ice the player hops onto to cross the open water. It has to read as
calm, flat, solid footing, and stay clearly distinct from the jagged, dangerous
ice hazard, so it is its own asset-generation case.

A model draws it as a single 32×32 sprite, one operation at a time: an
irregular, angular slab with the jagged edges of real fractured sea ice, filling
most of the tile, with a snow-dusted top surface, a ringed edge, and a waterline
shadow so it reads as floating, on full transparency.

The recorded operations are regenerated into the sprite. A reviewer judges it
against the brief, weighing the read as flat, calm, safe footing and not a
hazard, the floating waterline, and the pale ice palette.
