**Retro Arcade Loop** is a short, upbeat background music loop for a fast-paced
retro arcade action game — the cue that drives a high-score run.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a seamlessly looping clip
using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: an **upbeat, fast,
adrenal chiptune** cue — a bright bouncy square lead over a punchy synth bass and a
quick electronic drum groove, with snappy pluck and FM-bell counter-melodies
keeping the momentum up. The mood and role are prescribed; the key, tempo,
structure, and instrument choices are the model's, so the case rewards
compositional creativity. It is voiced as notes on instrument tracks the model
chooses freely from the baked `synthwave` instrument bank. The recorded operations
are regenerated into the clip (a stereo `clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`),
which a reviewer plays against the brief — its arcade energy, its chiptune flavor,
its driving groove, and its clean loop are what they weigh.
