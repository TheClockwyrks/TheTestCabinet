**Forest Prop Set** is a reusable pack of woodland scenery decorations — the kind of
nature props a level designer scatters through a forest scene. Because a usable set
has to hold together as one matched style, it is drawn as a **single 96×96 sprite**,
one operation at a time: eight storybook forest props laid out within the frame — a
round leafy tree, a pine tree, a leafy bush, a cluster of grass tufts, a mossy
boulder, a red mushroom cluster, a small flower patch, and a tree stump — each with
soft shading and a crisp silhouette on full transparency. The recorded operations are
regenerated into the sheet, which a reviewer judges against the brief: whether all
eight props are present and clearly readable, whether they share one cohesive
storybook look, and whether the named palette and clean layout are respected.
