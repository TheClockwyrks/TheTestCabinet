Introduced, in three upgrade tiers. The assembler is drawn at a base **amber**
tier, a faster **red-orange** reinforced tier, and a fastest **blue** advanced
tier, for twenty-four frames in all. The tiers share one grey-blue chassis,
footprint, and teal working state, differing only in hazard accent color,
mechanical detail, and craft-loop playback speed, so they read as one machine
progressively upgraded.
