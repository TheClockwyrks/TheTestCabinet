**Thunderhead Theme** is the main-menu music for *Thunderhead*, a real-time,
combined-arms fleet-command game fought across a cloud sea.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a long, grand,
cinematic main-title overture using only the `music` sequencer, one operation
at a time. The piece plays over the title screen. It is 3 to 5 minutes long,
must develop across that length rather than loop one idea, must loop cleanly
under the menu, and is not tied to any single power. The mood is a cold, high
war over an endless cloud sea: evocative, serious, and stirring, voiced as
notes on instrument tracks the model chooses freely from the `gm-lite`
instrument bank. The recorded operations are regenerated into the clip: a
stereo `clip.wav` and a portable `clip.mid`. A reviewer plays it against the
brief, weighing its grandeur, its development over the piece, and its clean
loop.
