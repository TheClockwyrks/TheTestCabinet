Introduced, in three upgrade tiers. The inserter is drawn at a base **amber** tier,
a faster **red-orange** reinforced tier, and a fastest **blue-cyan** advanced tier,
for thirty-six frames in all. The tiers share one grey-blue pivot base, reach, and
item slot, differing only in arm accent color, mechanical detail, and swing
playback speed, so they read as one inserter progressively upgraded.
