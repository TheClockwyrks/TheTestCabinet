**Floe Bear** is the hunter of the Floe arctic-crossing game, the huge white
predator that runs the player down on the pack ice and swims after them through
the open water. The pursuit is the game's signature. The bear has to read as
dangerous from every facing and stay visible against pale ice and under water.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a sprite sheet of 18
separate 32×32 frames, all from a top-down camera, one operation at a time: a
heavy, dark-outlined white bear with a four-direction run cycle, a
four-direction mostly-submerged swim with a trailing wake, and an open-maw
lunge. It is a creature on full transparency, composited onto the ice and water
by the game.

The recorded operations are regenerated into each frame, which a reviewer judges
against the brief: the read as a big dangerous animal, the consistent bulk
across facings, the four-way run, the readable four-direction submerged swim,
and the lunge. The named sequences play back as live animations in the review
UI.
