**Epic Boss Battle** is a cinematic combat cue for a towering final-boss
encounter — the music that plays while the player fights the biggest, most
dangerous adversary in the game.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, clean-looping
clip using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: a **dark, driving,
menacing** orchestral theme — a relentless low taiko pulse under aggressive
low-brass stabs and tense tremolo strings, a biting pizzicato ostinato, ominous
choir swells, and cymbal accents on the turns — high-stakes and propulsive, charged
with dread and adrenaline. The mood and role are prescribed; the key, tempo,
structure, and instrument choices are the model's, so the case rewards
compositional creativity. It is voiced as notes on instrument tracks the model
chooses freely from the baked `cinematic` instrument bank. The recorded operations
are regenerated into the clip (a stereo `clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`),
which a reviewer plays against the brief — its dark drive, its high-stakes tension,
and its relentless pulse are what they weigh.
