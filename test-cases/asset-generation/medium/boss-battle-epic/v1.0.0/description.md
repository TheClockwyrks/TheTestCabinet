**Epic Boss Battle** is a cinematic combat cue for a towering final-boss
encounter, the music that plays while the player fights the biggest, most
dangerous adversary in the game.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, clean-looping
clip using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: a dark,
driving, menacing orchestral theme built from a relentless low taiko pulse
under aggressive low-brass stabs and tense tremolo strings, a biting pizzicato
ostinato, ominous choir swells, and cymbal accents on the turns, high-stakes
and propulsive, charged with dread and adrenaline. The mood and role are
prescribed; the key, tempo, structure, and instrument choices are the model's,
so the case rewards compositional creativity. The theme is voiced as notes on
instrument tracks the model chooses freely from the `cinematic` instrument
bank. The recorded operations are regenerated into the clip, a stereo
`clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`. A reviewer plays it against the brief
and weighs its dark drive, its high-stakes tension, and its relentless pulse.
