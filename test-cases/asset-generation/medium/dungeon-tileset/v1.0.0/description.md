**Dungeon Tileset** is a reusable top-down dungeon-crawler building kit drawn as a
**single 96×96 sprite** — a 3×3 grid of nine 32×32 tiles a level editor would snap
together: plain, cracked, and mossy stone floors; a brick wall front-face and its
wall-top cap; a banded wooden door; stairs descending into the dark; a bone- and
rubble-strewn floor; and a lit floor brazier. A model builds it one drawing
operation at a time, in a small palette of dank stone tones with a single warm
brazier accent, on full transparency. The recorded operations are regenerated into
the sprite, which a reviewer judges against the brief: whether it reads as nine
distinct dungeon tiles, whether each tile depicts its subject, whether the floors
tile edge-to-edge, and whether the set holds together as one cold stone place lit by
the lone brazier.
