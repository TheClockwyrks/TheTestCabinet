**Wireworm Glitch** is the corrupted skittering foe of the Wireworm
circuit-board arcade game, the unstable sprite that roams the player's band
eating the board's capacitor nodes. Its whole character is instability.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a sprite sheet of 4
separate 32×32 frames, one operation at a time: an angular error-red mass split
by cyan/magenta chromatic-aberration fringes, torn by scanline breaks, and
dotted with white static, jittered to a different corruption in each frame. It
is corruption on full transparency, which the game composites onto the dark
board.

The recorded operations are regenerated into each frame, and the named sequence
plays back as a live animation in the review UI. A reviewer judges the frames
against the brief, weighing the "hostile error" read, the flickering instability
across the loop, and the tight glitch palette.
