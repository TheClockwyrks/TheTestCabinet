**Floe Plow** is a hazard of the Floe arctic-crossing game — a big, long tracked
snow plow that grinds back and forth across the lanes the player must thread.
Because it has to read instantly as a large, dangerous road machine to dodge (never
a creature or a safe platform), it is its own asset-generation case: a model draws
it as a **single 96×32 (three-tile) sprite**, one operation at a time — a
hard-edged steel plow on caterpillar treads, with a tall hazard-yellow plow blade
at its leading edge, a glass-windowed cab, and a red warning beacon, facing
right on
full transparency. The recorded operations are regenerated into the sprite,
which a
reviewer judges against the brief: the read as a big, long industrial hazard, the
snow-plow detailing, and the industrial palette are what they weigh.
