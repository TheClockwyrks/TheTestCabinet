**Folk Village Theme** is a warm pastoral acoustic-folk cue for a peaceful village
or town — gentle ambient music for a sunlit square.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, clean-looping clip
using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: a **warm, pastoral, cozy**
acoustic-folk theme — a fingerpicked nylon-guitar foundation under a lilting woodwind
melody, soft sustained strings beneath, and a gentle hand-percussion pulse — the
sound of community, comfort, and home. The mood and role are prescribed; the key,
tempo, structure, and instrument choices are the model's, so the case rewards
compositional creativity. It is voiced as notes on instrument tracks the model
chooses freely from the baked `gm-lite` instrument bank. The recorded operations are
regenerated into the clip (a stereo `clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`), which a
reviewer plays against the brief — its warm acoustic character, its cozy lilt, and
its clean loop are what they weigh.
