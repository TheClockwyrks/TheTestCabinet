**Cinematic Trailer Cue** is an epic orchestral teaser — the kind of grand, heroic
music that scores a blockbuster trailer.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short clip using only
the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: about **30 seconds** that open
sparse and tense — a lone voice over hushed tremolo strings and low choir — layer in
rising staccato and pizzicato ostinatos and soaring french horns over thunderous
taiko and bass-drum hits, and crest on a full choir-and-brass climax before a short
resolving tail. The cue builds to an ending; it need not loop. The mood and dynamic
arc are prescribed; the key, tempo, structure, and instrument choices are the
model's, so the case rewards compositional creativity. It is voiced as notes on
instrument tracks the model chooses freely from the baked `cinematic` instrument
bank. The recorded operations are regenerated into the clip (a stereo `clip.wav`
beside a portable `clip.mid`), which a reviewer plays against the brief — its
heroic sweep, its rising build, and the impact of its climax are what they weigh.
