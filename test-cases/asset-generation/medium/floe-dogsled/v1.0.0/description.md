**Floe Dogsled** is a hazard of the Floe arctic-crossing game, a team of husky
sled dogs pulling a musher's sled at speed across the lanes the player must
thread. It is the quick and light counterpart to the big slow snow plow, and it
has to read as a fast-moving vehicle to dodge.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a single 64×32 (two-tile)
sprite, one operation at a time: two running huskies leading at the right, a
taut harness back to a low sled on runners with a warm-parka musher, and a
little snow spray, racing right on full transparency. The recorded operations
are regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges against the brief: the
read as a fast sled team in motion, the huskies-plus-sled-plus-musher detailing,
and the arctic palette.
