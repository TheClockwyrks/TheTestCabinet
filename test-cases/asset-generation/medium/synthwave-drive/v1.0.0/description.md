**Synthwave Night Drive** is a retro-80s synthwave "night drive" loop — a short,
atmospheric cue built to cruise on repeat: an empty highway at night, city lights,
chrome and neon.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, seamlessly
looping clip using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: a **neon,
cruising, wistful** cue over a four-on-the-floor electronic drum-machine backbeat —
a pulsing arpeggiated bass, warm analog pads, a nostalgic saw/square lead melody,
and glassy FM-bell accents. The mood and role are prescribed; the key, tempo,
structure, and instrument choices are the model's, so the case rewards
compositional creativity. It is voiced as notes on instrument tracks the model
chooses freely from the baked `synthwave` instrument bank. The recorded operations
are regenerated into the clip (a stereo `clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`),
which a reviewer plays against the brief — its neon 80s textures, its driving
groove, and its wistful cool are what they weigh.
