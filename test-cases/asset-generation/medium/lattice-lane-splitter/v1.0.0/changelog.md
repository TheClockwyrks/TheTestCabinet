Introduced.

- **A lane splitter (unzipper), distinct from the plain splitter.** Instead of a
  flat lid hiding a side-to-side balancing shuttle, this machine carries a lengthwise
  East-West splitting ridge down its centre and two spreader heads that ride outward
  toward the outer lanes, so it reads as pulling the two streams apart — routing each
  belt out to the outer lane of its output — rather than balancing them.
- **The mechanism is a separate layer over a continuous belt bed.** The housing,
  output arrow, splitting ridge, and moving spreader heads are drawn on a registered
  `mechanism` layer, and the transport-belt surface runs unbroken beneath it
  (including under the machine) instead of stopping at the mouths. So an item rides
  the belt and passes *under* the mechanism, which the renderer can composite over
  items rather than fading them out at the machine.
