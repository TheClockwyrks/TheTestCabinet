**Floe Car** is a hazard of the Floe arctic-crossing game, an ordinary sedan
driving across the lanes the player must thread. It is the third vehicle
alongside the big slow snow plow and the fast sled-dog team, and it has to read
as a normal car to dodge.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a single 64×32 (two-tile)
sprite, one operation at a time: a top-down sedan with a rounded body, a glass
cabin, four wheels, and its head- and taillights, facing right on full
transparency. The recorded operations are regenerated into the sprite, which a
reviewer judges against the brief: the read as a plain car, the top-down sedan
detailing, and the car palette.
