**Ambient Exploration** is a calm, spacious cue for a quiet moment of discovery —
the sound of drifting through a vast, still landscape and taking it in.

This asset-generation case asks a model to compose it as a short, seamlessly
looping clip using only the `music` sequencer, one operation at a time: a **slow,
evolving** bed of synth pads and strings with sparse piano or vibraphone motifs
floating over the top — **serene, mysterious, and full of gentle wonder**, with
minimal or no percussion. The mood and role are prescribed; the key, tempo,
structure, and instrument choices are the model's, so the case rewards
compositional creativity. It is voiced as notes on instrument tracks the model
chooses freely from the baked `gm-lite` instrument bank. The recorded operations
are regenerated into the clip (a stereo `clip.wav` beside a portable `clip.mid`),
which a reviewer plays against the brief — its calm and space, its wonder and
mystery, and its clean loop are what they weigh.
