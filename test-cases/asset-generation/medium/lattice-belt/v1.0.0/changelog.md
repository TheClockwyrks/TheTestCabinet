Introduced, in three upgrade tiers. Each of the belt's two forms — the straight
segment and the 90-degree curve — is drawn at a base **amber** tier, a faster
**red-orange** reinforced tier, and a fastest **blue-cyan** advanced tier, for
forty-eight frames in all. The tiers share one metal body, cross-section, and
construction language, differing only in accent color, mechanical detail, and loop
playback speed, so they read as one belt progressively upgraded.
