**Spectra Laser** is the player fighter's laser-fire sound effect from *Spectra*, a
two-band formation shooter — the shot the player hears constantly as the fighter
fires upward at a swarm of drones.

This asset-generation case asks a model to synthesize it as a short mono clip using
only the `sfx-synth` tool, one operation at a time, from **oscillators and noise
alone — no samples**: a bright, arcade-retro "pew" with a crisp transient attack, a
fast downward pitch sweep, and a quick decay, all tucked well inside an 800 ms cap.
The recorded operations are re-rendered to a `.wav`, which a reviewer plays against
the brief — whether it reads unmistakably as an energetic synthetic laser, sweeps
downward in pitch, and snaps and decays cleanly.
