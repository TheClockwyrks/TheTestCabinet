**Fathom Flarefish** is a deep-sea predator that is blind between flares and
hunts in the light of its own bright bloom, one of the chasers in a lightless
maze-chase game. This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a sprite
sheet using only the drawing tool, one operation at a time: 8 separate 32×32
frames holding a four-direction swim cycle.

The creature carries its dim flare organ on its body, the brightest fixed mark
on the orange silhouette. The flare bloom itself lights a far larger radius than
the sprite and is a separate effect asset, the Fathom Flare Bloom case.

The recorded operations are regenerated into each frame, and the named sequences
play back as live animations in the review UI. A reviewer judges the frames
against the brief, weighing the flare organ, the four readable directions, and
the orange palette.
