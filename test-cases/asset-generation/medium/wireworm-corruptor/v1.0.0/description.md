**Wireworm Corruptor** is the node-charging foe of the Wireworm circuit-board
arcade game — the low, many-legged crawler that scuttles across the top of the
board slamming every capacitor node it touches to a full critical charge. Because
its scuttle and its charge stinger define it, it is its own asset-generation case:
a model draws it as a **sprite sheet** of 4 separate 32×32 frames, one operation
at a time — a low, wide, toxic-green segmented crawler with a red-eyed head
and an amber charge stinger, its legs cycling through a steady scuttle. It is a
creature on full transparency, composited onto the dark board by the game. The
recorded
operations are regenerated into each frame, which a reviewer judges against the
brief: the leggy-crawler read (clearly not the data-worm), the amber stinger that
ties it to the board's critical nodes, the steady crawl, and the tight palette are
what they weigh, and the named sequence plays back as a live animation in the
review UI.
