**Wireworm Corruptor** is the node-charging foe of the Wireworm circuit-board
arcade game, the low, many-legged crawler that scuttles across the top of the
board slamming every capacitor node it touches to a full critical charge.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a sprite sheet of 4
separate 32×32 frames, one operation at a time: a low, wide, toxic-green
segmented crawler with a red-eyed head and an amber charge stinger, its legs
cycling through a steady scuttle. It is a creature on full transparency, which
the game composites onto the dark board.

The recorded operations are regenerated into each frame, and the named sequence
plays back as a live animation in the review UI. A reviewer judges the frames
against the brief, weighing the leggy-crawler read that separates it from the
data-worm, the amber stinger that ties it to the board's critical nodes, the
steady crawl, and the tight palette.
