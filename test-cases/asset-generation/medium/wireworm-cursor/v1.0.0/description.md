**Wireworm Cursor** is the player's craft in the Wireworm circuit-board arcade
game, the defrag cursor that patrols the bottom band and fires upward at the
descending data-worm. It must read instantly as the player's own ship.

This asset-generation case asks a model to draw it as a single 32×32 sprite, one
operation at a time: a symmetric, upward-pointing delta craft with a glowing
reactor core, a muzzle at its tip, selection-caret corner ticks, and a thruster
underglow, in a cool cyan-and-white palette on full transparency.

The recorded operations are regenerated into the sprite, which a reviewer judges
against the brief. The upward-pointing player read, the friendly cursor motif,
and the tight palette are what they weigh.
