**Siege** is a first-person, voxel **last-stand survival** shooter for the browser.
You are a Warden defending a chain of three fortified redoubts — **A, B, and C** —
strung across a procedurally generated frontier, while the **Scourge**, a red
mechanized tide, pours in from the far edge to overrun them. You cannot win by
holding: the assault only escalates. Every redoubt is ground down and falls, you
fall back to the next, and when the last one breaks the siege is over. What you
play for is **how long you survive** and **how many attackers you destroy**.

You fight on foot, choosing one of three **classes** — a Ranger with an automatic
rifle, a Marksman with an armor-piercing long rifle, or a Breacher with an arcing
splash launcher — each with a primary, secondary, and grenade. A four-Warden
**squad** of two riflemen and two medics fights beside you, and **only the medics
can heal** — there are no pickups. The Scourge escalate on two axes: within a
redoubt's defense the same attackers climb tougher, up-armored **tiers**, and each
new redoubt introduces a **new kind of attacker** — while dedicated **breaker**
sappers and arcing **artillery** (with ground telegraphs you must dodge) grind each
redoubt down no matter how well you shoot.

Siege is a hard build: a procedurally generated voxel world on a real chunked mesh
renderer, a first-person pointer-lock controller, hitscan, projectile, and arcing
weapons, two independent 3D-pathfinding AI systems, a health-based objective with a
fighting-retreat survival loop, and multiple states. It is inspired by
objective-defense and horde survival shooters but is its own game, with an original
name, factions, world, and rosters.
