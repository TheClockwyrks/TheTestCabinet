# v0.39.0

<!-- cspell:words Nyxsis nyxsis -->

## What the update brings

The issues board under `tasks/` is retired in favour of Nyxsis's issues server,
`nyxsis-issues`, which files each issue as one file under `.nyxsis/issues/` and
holds its state in the file's frontmatter.

- `.nyxsis/project.toml` declares the clean command alone, with no `[issues]`
  table, and the render check holds it to that.
- `tasks/` is no longer rendered.
- `.claude/hooks/block-done-task-writes.sh` and its `.test.sh` are removed,
  with their registration in `.claude/settings.json` and their row in
  `.claude/README.md`: they guarded the board's `done/` folders, which no
  longer exist.
- The `repo-tasks` skill states how an issue is filed, started, blocked,
  released and closed through the issues server's tools, and that the design is
  edited in the same commit as the filing. Its sections on epic folders,
  `done/` and `blocked/` folders and kebab-case filenames are gone.
- The `drain-issues-queue` skill reads the open issues from the server's `list`
  tool, blocks an issue with the `block` tool carrying the reason, and closes a
  complete one with the `close` tool carrying its outcome, committed with the
  work.
- The `nyxsis` skill states no `[issues]` table.
- The markdownlint and cspell hooks of `.pre-commit-config.yaml` no longer name
  `^tasks/.*\.md$`.
- The layout tables of `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` carry no `tasks/` row, and
  `CLAUDE.md` points its issue-filing lines at the `repo-tasks` skill.
- The `tasks-migration` skill, at `.claude/skills/tasks-migration/SKILL.md`, is
  the guide a workspace rendered before this version follows to move off its
  board.

## Questions

It stops asking `issues_format`. An answers file recording it drops the answer
on its update, and no answer replaces it.

## Steps by hand

- Follow the `tasks-migration` skill: file every issue still open under
  `tasks/` through the issues server, delete `tasks/`, and take its remaining
  steps for what the merge cannot carry, such as a hook pattern or a layout row
  the project edited.
- Where the project edited `.claude/settings.json`, delete the
  `block-done-task-writes.sh` registration and its `Write|Edit|NotebookEdit`
  matcher wherever the merge left them, and delete
  `.claude/hooks/block-done-task-writes.sh` and its `.test.sh` where the update
  kept an edited copy of either.
