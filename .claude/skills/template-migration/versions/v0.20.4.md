# v0.20.4

## What the update brings

A fix for the Android application. The page was drawn under the status bar with
its top row unreachable; the activity now lays the webview out between the
system bars from the window's insets and paints the bands the bars occupy with
`status_bar` and `navigation_bar` from `values/colors.xml` and
`values-night/colors.xml`, which are seeds so a project states the two as its
own page's colors.

## Questions

It adds no question and stops asking none, so every recorded answer stays as it
is.

## Steps by hand

- Where the project builds the Android application and owns its seeds, add the
  `status_bar` and `navigation_bar` colors to both `values/colors.xml` and
  `values-night/colors.xml` under the crate's `gen/android/app/src/main/res/`,
  as the project's own page's colors.
