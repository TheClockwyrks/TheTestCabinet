# v0.8.0

## What the update brings

`ci_registry` defaults to the organization's registry, `genyume.azurecr.io`, and
`image_registry` to `genyume.azurecr.io/<slug>`, and the render seeds both
registries' words into the workspace's dictionary. A workspace keeps the
registries its answers record, so an update changes nothing else in it.

## Questions

It adds and drops no question. `ci_registry` and `image_registry` change their
defaults alone; a recorded answer stays as it is.

## Steps by hand

There is no step to take by hand.
