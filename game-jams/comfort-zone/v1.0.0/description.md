**Comfort Zone** is a game jam — a test case with no specification. Instead of
handing the model a game to build to a bar, it hands over a single **theme** and
asks for a complete, playable, enjoyable game built around it, with the model
producing all of the game's own art, effects, and sound during the run.

The name is the cozy game's whole promise: a _comfort zone_ is a warm, low-pressure
place you are happy to stay in. That is the mood every entry has to capture — calm,
unhurried, and charming, the kind of small world a player sinks into and does not want
to leave. Two things are fixed: the **cozy feeling** (warm and low-pressure, with no
harsh stakes or stress — games like Stardew Valley are the mood to aim for, not a
design to copy), and **a small world to spend time in** (a little lived-in place with
characters, spots, and things to tend, make, or discover, whose whole charm the model
must build). What the player actually does in it — farming, decorating, crafting,
keeping a shop, caring for creatures, gentle exploring, or something else — is the
model's to choose.

Runs are judged the way a game jam is judged: a person plays the entry and grades it
on general categories — **Playability**, **Fun**, **Theme**, **Presentation**,
**Audio**, **Polish**, and **Creativity** — on a five-level scale (💩 → 💎), and
gives the game one overall grade. There is no pass/fail checklist and no reference
build to match; the only brief is _make something playable and enjoyable_.
