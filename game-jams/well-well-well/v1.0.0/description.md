**Well, Well, Well** is a game jam — a test case with no specification. Instead of
handing the model a game to build to a bar, it hands over a single **theme** and
asks for a complete, playable, enjoyable game of _any genre_ built around it, with
the model producing all of the game's own art, effects, and sound during the run.

The name is a gravity pun with a wink. A _gravity well_ is the dip a mass makes in
space — the pull that draws everything toward it — and "well, well, well" is the
sound of a curious discovery. It is not a spec: there is no required number of wells
and no fixed setting to reproduce. What the theme fixes is a feel and a force, and
every entry must commit to both: **gravity is the whole game**, the dominant force
that shapes how things move and how it feels to move them; and it plays out in **a
star system that won't hold still** — worlds with real mass, in motion, pulling on
each other and on whatever the player controls, so no path travels straight.
Everything else — genre, mechanics, look, and feel — is the model's to invent, with
no examples handed over to gravitate toward.

Runs are judged the way a game jam is judged: a person plays the entry and grades it
on general categories — **Playability**, **Fun**, **Theme**, **Presentation**,
**Audio**, **Polish**, and **Creativity** — on a five-level scale (💩 → 💎), and
gives the game one overall grade. There is no pass/fail checklist and no reference
build to match; the only brief is _make something playable and enjoyable_.
