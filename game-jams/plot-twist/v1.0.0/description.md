**Plot Twist** is a game jam — a test case with no specification. Instead of
handing the model a game to build to a bar, it hands over a single **theme** and
asks for a complete, playable, enjoyable game built around it, with the model
producing all of the game's own art, effects, and sound during the run.

The name sets the spirit: a _plot twist_ is the turn that makes a story worth telling.
This jam asks for a **narrative game** — a game told through story, carried by its
writing and characters. Two things are fixed. It is a **narrative game**, where the
story is the core and any systems serve it. And, the crux, it is built on **meaningful
choices that branch**: a story on its own is a book, not a game, so the player must make
real choices that genuinely shape how the story unfolds — steering the path, changing
what happens, and leading to outcomes or endings that actually differ. Cosmetic choices,
or choices that all funnel back to the same result, do not count. Everything else — the
kind of story, how it is presented (a visual novel, a text adventure, a choose-your-path,
or a form of its own), and how the branching is structured — is the model's to invent.

Runs are judged the way a game jam is judged: a person plays the entry and grades it
on general categories — **Playability**, **Fun**, **Theme**, **Presentation**,
**Audio**, **Polish**, and **Creativity** — on a five-level scale (💩 → 💎), and
gives the game one overall grade. There is no pass/fail checklist and no reference
build to match; the only brief is _make something playable and enjoyable_.
